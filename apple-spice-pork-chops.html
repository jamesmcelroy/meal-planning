Apple Spice Pork Chops

Skillet pork chops simmered with apples, onion, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

45 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 + 35 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 4 pork chops
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 Fuji apples, cut in wedges
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/8 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1 cup chicken broth

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium. Season the chops and cook 5-7 minutes per side until browned. Move to a plate.
  2. Add the onion, apples, and butter to the skillet and cook until the onion softens.
  3. Add the broth, brown sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Return the chops and their juices to the pan.
  4. Simmer about 10 minutes until the liquid has reduced by half, the apples are soft, and the pork is cooked through.
  5. Serve the chops topped with the apples, onion, and sauce.

Notes

A fall dinner. Serve with mashed potatoes or roasted sweet potatoes. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
