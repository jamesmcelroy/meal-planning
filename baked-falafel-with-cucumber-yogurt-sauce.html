Baked Falafel with Cucumber Yogurt Sauce

Chickpea falafel baked instead of fried, served with a cool cucumber-mint yogurt sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from foodnetwork.com).

40 minTotal time 4-6 servingsYield 20 min + 20 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley
  • 1/2 cup tahini
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup chickpea flour (or all-purpose flour)
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • Flaky salt
  • 2 cucumbers, chopped
  • 1 cup chopped fresh mint
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped (for the sauce)
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375F and line or spray a baking sheet.
  2. Pulse the chickpeas, cilantro, parsley, tahini, garlic, chili powder, coriander, and cumin in a food processor with the egg until it forms a coarse paste.
  3. Mix in the chickpea flour, then the lemon zest and juice.
  4. Scoop and shape into balls, flatten slightly on the baking sheet, sprinkle with salt, and bake about 20 minutes until golden and crisp.
  5. For the sauce, blend the cucumber, mint, and garlic, stir in the yogurt, and season with salt.
  6. Serve the falafel warm with the cucumber yogurt sauce, in pita or over a bowl of greens and rice.

Notes

Baked instead of fried, so it's a lighter weeknight option. Adapted from the recipe at foodnetwork.com.
