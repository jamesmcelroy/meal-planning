Banh Mi Bowls

Vietnamese-inspired pork meatballs over rice with quick-pickled carrots, cucumber, and cilantro. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

60 minTotal time 6 servingsYield 30 + 30 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 carrots, shredded
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup rice vinegar
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 inches fresh ginger, grated
  • 1/2 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 6 cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 1 cucumber, quartered and sliced
  • 2 jalapenos, sliced
  • 1/2 bunch cilantro
  • Soy sauce or sriracha mayo, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the rice vinegar, sugar, salt, and water in a small pot until the sugar dissolves. Combine with the shredded carrot and sliced onion and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 400F. Mix the ground pork, egg, breadcrumbs, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and green onions, and form into about 30 small meatballs on a lined, greased baking sheet.
  3. Bake the meatballs 25 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
  4. While the meatballs bake, slice the jalapenos and cucumber and pick the cilantro leaves.
  5. Build bowls with rice, meatballs, pickled vegetables, cucumber, jalapeno, and cilantro. Finish with soy sauce or sriracha mayo.

Notes

The pickled carrots keep in the fridge for several days, so they're easy to make ahead on a Sunday. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
