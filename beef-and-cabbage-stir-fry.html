Beef and Cabbage Stir Fry

A vegetable-heavy skillet with a little lean ground beef and a soy-sesame sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

30 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 15 + 15 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon sriracha (less for a mild version)
  • 1/2 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1/2 head green cabbage, shredded
  • 2 carrots, shredded
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 1/2 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1/2 lb lean ground beef
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • Salt and pepper
  • Sesame seeds, optional

Instructions

  1. Stir the soy sauce, sesame oil, sriracha, and brown sugar together.
  2. Shred the cabbage and carrots, slice the green onions, and mince the garlic and ginger.
  3. Brown the beef in the oil over medium heat with the garlic, ginger, salt, and pepper, about 5 minutes.
  4. Add the cabbage and carrots and cook until they start to wilt.
  5. Pour in the sauce, add the green onions, and toss. Top with sesame seeds.

Notes

Low-carb as written; serve over rice if you want it heartier. Cut the sriracha for a kid-friendly version. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
