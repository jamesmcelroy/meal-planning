Beef Bulgogi

Thin-sliced ribeye marinated in a sweet soy-sesame sauce and quickly seared. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from onceuponachef.com).

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 15 min + 10 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb ribeye steak, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons vegetable oil, plus more for cooking
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 small shallot, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh ginger
  • Pinch of crushed red pepper flakes
  • Sliced scallions and sesame seeds, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Blend the soy sauce, brown sugar, oil, sesame oil, garlic, shallot, ginger, and red pepper flakes into a smooth marinade.
  2. Toss the sliced beef with the marinade, cover, and refrigerate at least 1 hour (or overnight for more flavor).
  3. Heat a little oil in a large skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the beef in batches in a single layer, about 2-3 minutes per side, until browned and just cooked through. Keep warm between batches.
  5. Garnish with scallions and sesame seeds, and serve with rice.

Notes

Marinate in the morning (or the night before) so dinner is just a quick sear. Adapted from the recipe at onceuponachef.com.
