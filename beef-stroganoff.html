Beef Stroganoff

A one-skillet, from-scratch beef stroganoff with mushrooms and egg noodles. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

30 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 + 20 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 yellow onion, diced small (optional)
  • 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1/8 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 8 oz wide egg noodles
  • 1/3 cup sour cream
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley (optional)

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat and saute the garlic (and onion, if using) about 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  3. Add the sliced mushrooms and black pepper and cook about 5 minutes until the mushrooms soften.
  4. Stir in the uncooked egg noodles, beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, and Dijon mustard.
  5. Cover, bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to low and simmer 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the noodles are tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.
  6. Stir in the sour cream until creamy, adjust seasoning, and garnish with parsley.

Notes

Everything cooks in one pot, noodles included — easy cleanup. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
