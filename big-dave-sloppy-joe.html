Big Dave Sloppy Joe

Imported from the family's AnyList recipe collection.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tbsp cider vinnegar
  • 2 tbsp worcestershire
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 3/4 cup ketchup
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1.5 lb hamburger
  • 8 hamburger buns

Instructions

  1. fry ground beef, drain
  2. add ketchup, water, chili powder, cider vinegar, worcestershire sauce, pepper
  3. cook uncovered over medium heat sittring occasionally until thick (about 20 minutes or unti lmost liquid has evaporated and mixture if thick)
  4. toast buns
