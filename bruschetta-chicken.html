Bruschetta Chicken

Thin chicken cutlets under melted mozzarella with a fresh tomato-basil topping. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 + 15 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 4 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons finely diced red onion
  • 2 tablespoons thinly sliced fresh basil
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon sugar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast (2 breasts)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic glaze

Instructions

  1. Mix the tomatoes, garlic, red onion, basil, 1 tablespoon olive oil, balsamic vinegar, sugar, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Set aside.
  2. Slice each chicken breast in half horizontally to make 4 thin cutlets. Pat dry and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Heat the remaining olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook the chicken 3-5 minutes per side, until golden and 165°F inside.
  4. Turn the heat to low, top each cutlet with mozzarella, cover, and cook 2-4 minutes until melted.
  5. Spoon the tomato mixture over the chicken and drizzle with balsamic glaze.

Notes

Good with a green salad or steamed green beans. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
