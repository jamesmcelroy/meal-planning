Burgers & Bagged Salad

Classic weeknight burgers — juicy, fast, and endlessly topping-friendly.

20 minTotal time 4 servingsYield EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef (80/20) or ground turkey
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar or American cheese
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 bag pre-washed salad greens
  • Your favorite salad dressing

Instructions

  1. Combine ground beef, garlic powder, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Mix gently — overworking makes tough burgers. Form into 4 patties about 3/4 inch thick. Press a shallow dimple in the center of each.
  2. Heat a cast iron skillet or grill pan over high heat until very hot. Cook patties without moving them for 4 minutes.
  3. Flip once. Place a slice of cheese on top of each patty. Cook to desired doneness, about 3 more minutes. Medium is 160°F internal temp for beef.
  4. Toast buns cut-side down in the same pan for 1 minute. Assemble burgers with your preferred toppings.
  5. Toss salad greens with dressing and serve alongside.

Notes

The 80/20 beef ratio is key — the fat makes a burger juicy. Don't press down on the patty while cooking. The dimple in the center prevents it from puffing into a dome.
