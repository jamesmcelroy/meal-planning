Chicken Marsala

Thin chicken cutlets in a mushroom and Marsala wine pan sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from recipetineats.com).

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 min + 15 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 large boneless, skinless chicken breasts, halved horizontally into 4 thin cutlets
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 2 shallots, finely chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 2 cups sliced white mushrooms
  • 1 cup dry Marsala wine
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • Chopped parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Pound the chicken cutlets to an even thickness, then season with salt and pepper and dust lightly with flour.
  2. Heat the olive oil and half the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken until golden and cooked through, then remove and set aside.
  3. Add the remaining butter to the pan and cook the shallots and garlic briefly, then add the mushrooms and cook until softened.
  4. Pour in the Marsala wine and simmer until it reduces by about half.
  5. Stir in the chicken broth and cream, and simmer until the sauce thickens slightly.
  6. Return the chicken to the pan to warm through, then garnish with parsley and serve over pasta, rice, or mashed potatoes.

Notes

Good over egg noodles or mashed potatoes with a side of green beans. Adapted from the recipe at recipetineats.com.
