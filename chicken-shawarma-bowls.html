Chicken Shawarma Bowls

Warm spiced chicken thighs over a crisp herby salad with lemon and hummus. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from themediterraneandish.com).

30 minTotal time 6 servingsYield 15 + 15 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 8 boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 3/4 tablespoon ground cumin
  • 3/4 tablespoon turmeric
  • 3/4 tablespoon ground coriander
  • 3/4 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 3/4 tablespoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • Juice of 1 lemon, plus more for the dressing
  • 1/3 cup olive oil, plus more for the skillet
  • 8 oz baby arugula
  • 2-3 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 English cucumber, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon sumac
  • Hummus or tzatziki, for serving

Instructions

  1. Stir together the cumin, turmeric, coriander, garlic powder, paprika, cloves, cayenne, and salt in a small bowl.
  2. Toss the chicken with the spice mix, sliced onion, lemon juice, and 1/3 cup olive oil. Let sit while you prep the salad.
  3. Combine the arugula, tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion in a large bowl.
  4. Whisk a little more lemon juice with a drizzle of olive oil, the minced garlic, sumac, salt, and pepper for the dressing.
  5. Heat a splash of olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the chicken 5-6 minutes, stir, then cook another 5-6 minutes until browned and cooked through.
  6. Toss the salad with the dressing, divide into bowls, top with the chicken, and serve with hummus or tzatziki.

Notes

Good with warm pita on the side. Adapted from the recipe at themediterraneandish.com.
