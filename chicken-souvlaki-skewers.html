Chicken Souvlaki Skewers

Lemon-oregano marinated chicken grilled on skewers with homemade tzatziki. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from onceuponachef.com).

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 15 min + 10 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 clove garlic, minced (for tzatziki)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (for tzatziki)
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill or mint
  • Lemon wedges and warm pita, for serving

Instructions

  1. Whisk the olive oil, garlic, oregano, honey, lemon zest, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Add the chicken, toss to coat, cover, and refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
  2. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 30 minutes. Thread the marinated chicken onto the skewers.
  3. Grill over medium-high heat, turning occasionally, about 5-7 minutes total, until the chicken is cooked through and lightly charred.
  4. While the chicken cooks, stir together the yogurt, cucumber, garlic, olive oil, dill, and a pinch of salt for the tzatziki.
  5. Serve the skewers with warm pita, lemon wedges, and the tzatziki on the side.

Notes

Marinate the chicken the night before so it's ready to grill on a busy weeknight. No grill? Use a grill pan or broiler, about 3-4 minutes per side. Adapted from the recipe at onceuponachef.com.
