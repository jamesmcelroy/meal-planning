Chicken Stir-Fry with Hoisin

A quick, veggie-packed stir-fry with a sweet-savory hoisin sauce, on the table fast.

22 minTotal time 4 servingsYield EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless chicken thighs or breast, sliced thin
  • 3 tablespoons hoisin sauce
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • Cooked rice, for serving
  • Sliced scallions, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Whisk together hoisin sauce, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add chicken and cook 5-6 minutes until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add remaining oil to the pan. Add broccoli, bell pepper, and carrot; stir-fry 4-5 minutes until crisp-tender. Add garlic and ginger in the last minute.
  4. Return chicken to the pan, pour sauce over everything, and toss to coat. Cook 1-2 minutes until sauce is heated through and slightly thickened.
  5. Serve over rice, garnished with scallions.

Notes

Swap in whatever vegetables are on hand — snap peas, mushrooms, and baby corn all work well. Pre-sliced stir-fry veggie bags save prep time.
