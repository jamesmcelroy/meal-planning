Classic Beef Chili

A straightforward stovetop chili with ground beef, two kinds of beans, and pantry spices. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

50 minTotal time 6 servingsYield 5 + 45 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 (15.5 oz) can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 (15.25 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Saute the onion and garlic in the olive oil over medium heat until soft and translucent.
  2. Add the ground beef, breaking it up as it cooks, until fully browned.
  3. Add both beans, the diced tomatoes, tomato paste, water, and all the seasonings. Stir until combined.
  4. Cover and simmer over low heat at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Taste and adjust seasoning, then serve with your favorite toppings.

Notes

Freezes well, so it's worth doubling for a future quick dinner. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
