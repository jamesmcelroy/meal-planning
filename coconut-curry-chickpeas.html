Coconut Curry Chickpeas

A fast, creamy chickpea curry with kale, simmered in coconut milk and warm spices. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

35 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 + 25 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  • 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
  • 4 oz kale, chopped (about 3 cups)
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Toast the curry powder and cumin in a large skillet over medium-low heat for about a minute.
  2. Add the onion, garlic, ginger, and olive oil, and saute 5 minutes until the onion softens.
  3. Add the chickpeas, tomato sauce, and coconut milk, and stir to combine.
  4. Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat, then reduce to medium-low and simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Stir in the kale and cook about 5 minutes until wilted. Season with salt and serve over rice or with bread.

Notes

One skillet, no meat, and leftovers reheat well for lunch. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
