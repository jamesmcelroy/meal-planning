Crab Cakes

Pan-fried crab cakes with Old Bay, Dijon, and a quick breadcrumb binder. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

20 minTotal time 4 crab cakesYield 10 min + 10 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 (6 oz) cans crab meat, drained
  • 1/4 cup finely diced yellow onion
  • 1/4 cup finely diced celery
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 teaspoons lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoons soy sauce
  • 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon Old Bay seasoning
  • 1/2 cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, for frying

Instructions

  1. Stir together the crab, onion, celery, garlic, mayonnaise, lemon juice, soy sauce, Dijon, and Old Bay in a bowl.
  2. Fold in the breadcrumbs until the mixture holds together, adding a little more if needed.
  3. Shape into 4 firm patties.
  4. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and fry the patties about 4-5 minutes per side, without moving them, until golden brown.
  5. Drain briefly on paper towels before serving.

Notes

Serve with a squeeze of lemon and a simple green salad. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
