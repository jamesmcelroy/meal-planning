Creamy Tomato Chicken

Seared chicken cutlets in a tomato-paste and milk sauce with fresh diced tomatoes. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

35 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 15 + 20 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts (about 1.5 lbs), sliced into 4 cutlets
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1/2 onion, small dice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 3 Roma tomatoes, diced

Instructions

  1. Slice each chicken breast lengthwise into two cutlets. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear the chicken in 2 tablespoons oil over medium-high, 4-5 minutes per side, until 165°F inside. Set aside.
  3. Lower the heat to medium. Cook the onion and garlic 3 minutes, then stir in the tomato paste for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Add the remaining oil and the flour and stir for 1 minute.
  5. Whisk in the milk, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and more salt and pepper. Simmer 2 minutes until thickened.
  6. Stir in the fresh tomatoes, return the chicken and its juices to the pan, and warm through.

Notes

Works over pasta, rice, or with crusty bread. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
