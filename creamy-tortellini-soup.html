Creamy Tortellini Soup

Imported from AnyList (originally from damndelicious.net).

30 minTotal time 4-6 servingsYield

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium sweet onion, diced
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 2 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 (8-oz) can tomato sauce
  • 1 (9-oz) pkg refrigerated three cheese tortellini
  • 1/2 bunch kale, stems removed and leaves chopped
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 3 Tbsp chopped fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large stockpot or Dutch oven over medium heat.
  2. Add Italian sausage (casing removed) and cook until browned, about 3-5 minutes, making sure to crumble the sausage as it cooks; drain excess fat.
  3. Stir in garlic, onion and Italian seasoning.
  4. Cook, stirring frequently, until onions have become translucent, about 2-3 minutes; season with salt and pepper, to taste.
  5. Whisk in flour until lightly browned, about 1 minute.
  6. Gradually whisk in chicken stock and tomato sauce.
  7. Bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, until reduced and slightly thickened, about 10 minutes.
  8. Stir in tortellini; cover and cook until tender, about 5-7 minutes.
  9. Stir in kale until wilted, about 1-2 minutes.
  10. Stir in heavy cream and basil until heated through, about 1 minute; season with salt and pepper, to taste.
  11. Serve immediately.
