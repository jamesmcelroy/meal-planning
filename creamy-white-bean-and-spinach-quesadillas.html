Creamy White Bean and Spinach Quesadillas

Quesadillas filled with seasoned cannellini beans, spinach, pepper jack, and sour cream. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

20 minTotal time 4 quesadillasYield 10 + 10 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 (15 oz) can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3/4 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/8 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups fresh spinach, chopped
  • 4 oz pepper jack cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 4 (8-inch) flour tortillas
  • Salsa, to serve

Instructions

  1. Mix the beans with the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and salt.
  2. Stir in the spinach, cheese, and sour cream.
  3. Spread the filling over half of each tortilla and fold over.
  4. Cook in a dry skillet over medium heat, 3-5 minutes per side, until browned and crisp.
  5. Cut in half and serve with salsa.

Notes

Swap in plain Monterey Jack for a milder version. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
