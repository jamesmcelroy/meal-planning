Cuban Mojo Pork Tenderloin

Pork tenderloin marinated in a citrus-garlic mojo sauce and grilled. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from the American Heart Association's recipes.heart.org); we swapped their fried plantains for rice and black beans.

35 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 15 min + 20 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 lb pork tenderloin, trimmed
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup fresh lime juice
  • 8 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Black pepper
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons oil, for grilling
  • Rice and black beans, for serving

Instructions

  1. Blend the cilantro, orange juice, green onions, lemon juice, lime juice, garlic, cumin, and oregano until smooth. Set aside about 1/4 cup of the mixture to serve with the pork later.
  2. Pour the rest over the pork tenderloin and marinate, covered, in the fridge for 2-24 hours.
  3. Heat a grill to medium-high. Remove the pork from the marinade (discard that marinade), season with pepper, and brush lightly with oil.
  4. Grill the pork, turning occasionally, about 15-20 minutes, until it reaches 145F in the center. Let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  5. Spoon the reserved marinade over the sliced pork and serve with rice and black beans or roasted vegetables.

Notes

The original recipe serves this with fried plantains; we swapped in rice and black beans for a lighter weeknight side. Adapted from the recipe at recipes.heart.org.
