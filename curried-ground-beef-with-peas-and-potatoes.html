Curried Ground Beef with Peas and Potatoes

A mild curried skillet of ground beef, potatoes, and peas that makes good leftovers. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

35 minTotal time 6 servingsYield 10 + 25 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper (optional)
  • 1 lb ground beef (15% fat or less)
  • 1 russet potato (about 3/4 lb), cut in 1/2-inch cubes
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Cook the garlic and ginger in the oil over medium heat for 1-2 minutes. Add the curry powder, turmeric, and red pepper and cook 1 minute more.
  2. Add the beef and cook until browned. Cube the potato while it cooks.
  3. Add the potato and broth, cover, and bring to a boil. Reduce to medium-low and simmer covered 10 minutes until the potato is tender.
  4. Uncover and simmer until most of the broth has cooked off.
  5. Stir in the peas, season with salt, and top with green onions.

Notes

Skip the red pepper for a mild version. Serve with rice or naan. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
