Dan Dan Noodles

Spaghetti with ground pork, spinach, and a soy, peanut butter, and vinegar sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

30 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 + 20 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons grated fresh ginger
  • 1 cup frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce (for the sauce)
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons creamy peanut butter
  • 2 tablespoons chili crisp or chili oil (less for kids)
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar (for the sauce)
  • 1 lb thin spaghetti
  • 6 green onions, sliced
  • 1/3 cup dry roasted peanuts, crushed

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water until just shy of done. Save 1 cup pasta water, then drain.
  2. Brown the pork in the oil over medium-high, pressing it down to get a crust.
  3. Stir in the garlic, ginger, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 1 tablespoon brown sugar, and spinach until hot. Turn off the heat.
  4. Whisk the sauce ingredients with half the pasta water until smooth.
  5. Toss the noodles in the sauce, adding pasta water as needed to loosen it.
  6. Top with the pork, green onions, and peanuts.

Notes

Reduce or skip the chili crisp for a mild version. Contains peanuts, which is fine with no allergies in the house. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
