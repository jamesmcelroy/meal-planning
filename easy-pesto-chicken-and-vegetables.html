Easy Pesto Chicken and Vegetables

One-skillet chicken and summer vegetables tossed with store-bought pesto. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

30 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 15 + 15 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 zucchini, chopped
  • 1 yellow squash, chopped
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced
  • 1.3 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 cup frozen green beans
  • 1/3 cup pesto
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tablespoon grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Chop the bell pepper, zucchini, and squash into similar-sized pieces. Slice the onion and cube the chicken.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook the chicken 3-5 minutes until no longer pink outside.
  3. Add the frozen green beans and cook 1-2 minutes. Add the bell pepper and onion and cook 1-2 minutes more.
  4. Add the zucchini and squash and cook 2-3 minutes until slightly softened.
  5. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the pesto, season to taste, and top with Parmesan.

Notes

Serve as is, or over rice or pasta. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
