Fiesta Chicken Fajitas

Imported from the family's AnyList recipe collection.

26 minTotal time

Ingredients

  • 1.25 lb boneless chicken breasts
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 2 Tbsp oil
  • 1 medium onion
  • 1/2 cup red pepper
  • 1/2 cup green pepper
  • 1/2 cup yellow pepper
  • 1 medium onion
  • 1/4 cup salsa
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 1/5 cups shredded cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. cut chicken into strips
  2. mince garlic
  3. marinate chicken in guice, garlic, and seasonings 15 minutes
  4. warm fajitas
  5. cut onion into thin wedges
  6. cut peppers into strips
  7. cook chicken in oil 2-3 minute until loses pink color
  8. add onions and peppers
  9. continue cooking until tneder
  10. stir in salsa
  11. spoon onto tortillas
  12. top with cheese
