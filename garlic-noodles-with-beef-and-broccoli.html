Garlic Noodles with Beef and Broccoli

Spaghetti tossed with ground beef, frozen broccoli, and an oyster-sauce garlic sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 + 15 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 3 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 8 oz spaghetti
  • 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 lb frozen broccoli florets
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • Crushed red pepper, optional

Instructions

  1. Melt 2 tablespoons butter and whisk it with the oyster sauce, soy sauce, brown sugar, and sesame oil.
  2. Cook the spaghetti in boiling water until tender, about 7 minutes. Drain.
  3. Melt the remaining butter in a large skillet over medium and brown the beef, 5-7 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed.
  4. Add the garlic for 1-2 minutes, then the frozen broccoli until no longer frozen.
  5. Add the pasta and sauce, toss until coated and hot, and top with green onions and red pepper if you like.

Notes

Skip the red pepper for kids. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
