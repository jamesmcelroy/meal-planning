Glazed Pork Chops

Thick pork chops rubbed with a brown sugar and paprika mix, seared, and finished in the pan sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 + 15 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne (use less for a milder chop)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 thick-cut boneless pork chops

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Mix the brown sugar and spices.
  2. Rub the mix over all sides of the chops.
  3. Heat the oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium and cook the chops about 5 minutes per side until browned.
  4. If the chops are over 1 inch thick, move the skillet to the oven for 5-10 minutes. Check that they reach 145°F.
  5. Spoon the pan sauce over the chops before serving.

Notes

Pairs with roasted potatoes and a green vegetable. Use a meat thermometer so the chops don't dry out. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
