Korean Gochujang Beef Bowls

Quick spicy-sweet ground beef and peppers over rice with a cool cucumber salad. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from halfbakedharvest.com).

20 minTotal time 6 servingsYield 10 + 10 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs ground beef
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 3 shallots, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 2-3 tablespoons gochujang (Korean chili paste), plus 1 tablespoon more for the cucumber salad
  • 2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup toasted sesame seeds
  • 1/3 cup chopped roasted peanuts
  • Cooked rice, for serving
  • 4 Persian cucumbers, chopped
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onion
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat, about 5 minutes. Add the butter, bell peppers, shallots, garlic, and ginger, and cook 2 minutes until fragrant.
  2. Stir in the soy sauce, 2-3 tablespoons gochujang, and a splash of water. Cook 2-3 minutes until the sauce coats the beef, then stir in the honey and cook 1-2 more minutes until caramelized. Toss with the sesame seeds.
  3. Toss the chopped cucumbers with a pinch of salt, the remaining tablespoon of gochujang, green onion, and rice vinegar. Let sit 5 minutes.
  4. Serve the beef and peppers over rice, topped with the cucumber salad and peanuts.

Notes

One of the fastest dinners in this collection — done in 20 minutes flat. Adapted from the recipe at halfbakedharvest.com.
