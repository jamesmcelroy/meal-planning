Lemon Baked Cod

Flaky cod baked under a garlicky lemon-parsley butter - ready in under 30 minutes. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from thatlowcarblife.com).

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 5 + 20 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 4 cod fillets (about 4 oz each)
  • 1/4 cup butter, softened
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon minced parsley
  • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 thin lemon slices

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400F and coat a rimmed baking sheet with nonstick spray.
  2. Mash together the butter, lemon juice, parsley, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread the butter mixture over each cod fillet and top with a lemon slice.
  4. Bake about 15 minutes, until the cod is opaque and flakes easily with a fork.

Notes

Good with roasted sweet potatoes or a quick salad on the side. Adapted from the recipe at thatlowcarblife.com.
