Mediterranean Pasta

Imported from AnyList (originally from landolakes.com).

15 minTotal time 4 servingsYield

Ingredients

  • 8 oz uncooked dried linguine pasta
  • 1 1/2 tsp lemon pepper seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 (5-oz) boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1/4 cup Land O Lakes® Butter with Olive Oil & Sea Salt
  • 1 Tbsp finely chopped fresh garlic
  • 1/2 cup Land O Lakes® Heavy Whipping Cream
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil leaves
  • 1/3 cup pitted kalamata olives, quartered
  • 1/4 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup feta cheese crumbles

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain; keep warm.
  2. Sprinkle lemon pepper seasoning and salt onto both sides of chicken breasts. Melt Butter with Olive Oil & Sea Salt in 10-inch skillet until sizzling; add chicken breasts. Cook over medium heat, turning once, 15-20 minutes or until internal temperature reaches 165°F, chicken is golden brown and no longer pink in center. Add garlic during last 2-3 minutes of cooking time.
  3. Add whipping cream and basil to chicken in skillet; stir to combine and coat chicken. Remove chicken breasts to cutting board; cut each into slices. Add olives and sun-dried tomatoes to cream sauce; cook 1-2 minutes or until mixture is bubbling and slightly thickened.
  4. Place pasta onto individual serving plates; top each with 1 sliced chicken breast. Pour sauce over chicken. Sprinkle each with feta cheese crumbles.
