Sheet Pan Sausage & Roasted Veggies

A hearty, hands-off weeknight dinner with minimal cleanup.

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 4 Italian sausage links
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Slice bell peppers, zucchini, and red onion. Spread on a large sheet pan.
  3. Drizzle olive oil over the vegetables. Sprinkle garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper. Toss to coat evenly.
  4. Nestle sausage links among the vegetables on the pan.
  5. Roast at 400°F for 20 minutes, until sausage is cooked through and veggies are caramelized at the edges.
  6. Slice sausages and serve directly from the pan.

Notes

Swap in any vegetables you have on hand — broccoli, mushrooms, or cherry tomatoes all work great. Pre-sliced stir-fry veggie bags from the store save even more time.
