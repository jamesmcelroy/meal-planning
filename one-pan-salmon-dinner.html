One Pan Salmon Dinner

Salmon baked in a parchment packet with a soy-brown sugar glaze, on one sheet pan with potatoes and green beans. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

40 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 15 + 25 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salmon (2 large fillets)
  • 2 Yukon gold potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon salt, divided
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon ginger powder
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1/2 lemon, sliced
  • 2 cups green beans
  • 1/2 teaspoon sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Thaw the salmon and heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Toss the potato slices with a little salt, pepper, and the olive oil. Spread on a parchment-lined sheet pan and bake 15 minutes.
  3. Mix the remaining salt, ginger, garlic powder, brown sugar, soy sauce, and sesame oil into a glaze.
  4. Set the salmon on a sheet of parchment, brush with glaze, top with lemon slices, and fold into a sealed packet.
  5. Toss the green beans with the remaining glaze, salt, and sesame seeds.
  6. Flip the potatoes, add the salmon packet and green beans to the pan, and bake 10-15 minutes until the salmon reaches 145°F.

Notes

Prep and bake fit within 40 minutes, but it is the longest of this week's batch. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
