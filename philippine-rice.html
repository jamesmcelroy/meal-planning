Philippine Rice

Imported from AnyList (originally from Ann Caldwell).

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ground Beef
  • 1 cup Green Bell Peppers, cut into cubes
  • 1 cup Carrots, cut into cubes
  • 1 cup Pepperoni, slices
  • 1/2 cup Onions, cut into cubes
  • 1 tsp Garlic, minced
  • 3 cups Rice, cooked
  • 3 Eggs

Instructions

  1. Fry three eggs beaten in large frying pan dice and add last
  2. Fry ground beef and drain then add onion, pepperoni, garlic, and a little salt to taste
  3. When Brown add pepper, carrots, and rice. Mix well
