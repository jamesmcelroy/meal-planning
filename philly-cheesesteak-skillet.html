Philly Cheesesteak Skillet

Shaved steak, peppers, onions, and mushrooms melted with provolone on toasted hoagie rolls. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

30 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 min + 20 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 8 oz baby bella mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil, divided
  • 12-14 oz thinly sliced ribeye (shaved steak)
  • Salt, garlic powder, and black pepper
  • 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 4-5 hoagie rolls
  • 6 slices provolone cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook the mushrooms in a large skillet with half the butter and oil until softened, then add the onion and pepper and cook until tender. Remove the vegetables from the pan.
  2. Add the remaining butter and oil to the skillet and brown the shaved steak.
  3. Return the vegetables to the pan with the steak, season with salt, garlic powder, pepper, and Worcestershire sauce, and stir to combine.
  4. Lay the provolone over the top, turn off the heat, and let it melt from the residual heat.
  5. Split and toast the hoagie rolls, then divide the filling among them.

Notes

Everything cooks in one skillet - the oven (or a toaster oven) is only needed to toast the rolls. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
