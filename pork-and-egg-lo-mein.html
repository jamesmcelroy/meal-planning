Pork and Egg Lo Mein

Stir-fried noodles with sliced pork, egg, and broccoli slaw in a soy-oyster sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from foodnetwork.com).

30 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 min + 20 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 6 oz dried lo mein noodles (or spaghetti)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 3/4 lb boneless pork chops, thinly sliced
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 bunch scallions, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon minced ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups broccoli slaw mix (or shredded cabbage and carrot)
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles in salted boiling water, drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside, reserving a few tablespoons of the cooking water.
  2. Stir the soy sauce and oyster sauce together, then toss the sliced pork with a spoonful of it.
  3. Cook the beaten eggs in a little oil as a thin omelet, then slide it out and slice into thin strips.
  4. Stir-fry the pork in more oil until mostly cooked, then set aside. Stir-fry the scallions, ginger, and garlic briefly, then add the broccoli slaw and bell pepper and cook a few minutes until just tender.
  5. Return the pork to the pan along with the noodles, reserved cooking water, and remaining sauce. Toss until heated through, then fold in the egg strips and serve.

Notes

A good way to use up a bag of broccoli slaw. Adapted from the recipe at foodnetwork.com.
