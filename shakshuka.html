Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a smoky, spiced tomato and pepper sauce - a quick meatless dinner. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

39 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 5 + 34 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 jalapenos, seeded and diced
  • 5 ribs celery, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 2 tablespoons smoked paprika
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons cardamom
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • Water, as needed
  • 3 eggs
  • Fresh parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350F. Heat the olive oil in an oven-safe skillet and add the onion, bell pepper, jalapeno, celery, and all the spices.
  2. Cook over medium heat 10-12 minutes until the vegetables are softened and fragrant.
  3. Stir in the garlic, diced tomatoes, tomato paste, lemon zest and juice, and sugar. Simmer 10 minutes, adding a splash of water if it gets too thick.
  4. Make small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Transfer to the oven and bake 8-12 minutes until the egg whites are set. Garnish with black pepper and parsley, and serve with pita.

Notes

Needs an oven-safe skillet since it finishes under the broiler-adjacent oven heat. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
