Sheet Pan Garlic Butter Shrimp

Shrimp roasted on one pan with garlic, then tossed in butter right on the hot sheet. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from onceuponachef.com).

20 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 5 + 8 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 2 lb extra large or jumbo shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 large cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
  • Chopped parsley and lemon wedges, to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F with a rack in the middle.
  2. Toss the shrimp on a baking sheet with the olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic.
  3. Roast about 8 minutes until pink and cooked through.
  4. Add the butter to the hot pan and stir until melted and coating the shrimp.
  5. Serve with parsley and lemon.

Notes

The source serves 4 to 6 with 2 lb of shrimp; halve it for a smaller family. Good over pasta or rice with a vegetable on the side. Adapted from the recipe at onceuponachef.com.
