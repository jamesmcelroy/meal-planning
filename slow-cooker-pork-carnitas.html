Slow-Cooker Pork Carnitas

Citrus-braised pork shoulder, shredded and crisped, for build-your-own tacos. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from foodnetwork.com).

5 hr 20 minTotal time 6 servingsYield 20 min + 5 hrPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs boneless pork shoulder, trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • Juice of 2 oranges
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 tablespoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • Pinch of ground cloves
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 medium white onion, quartered
  • 6 cloves garlic, peeled
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1/4 cup lard or oil, for crisping
  • Corn tortillas, cilantro, sliced jalapeno, and pickled red onion, for serving

Instructions

  1. Combine the broth, orange and lime juice, spices, salt, onion, garlic, and bay leaves in the slow cooker. Add the pork and turn to coat.
  2. Cover and cook on high about 5 hours, until the pork is fork-tender.
  3. Lift the pork out with a slotted spoon, discard the onion, garlic, and bay leaves, and shred the meat with two forks, keeping some larger chunks. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Heat the lard in a skillet over medium-high heat in two batches. Press half the shredded pork flat and cook 2-3 minutes until crisped at the edges. Repeat with the rest.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with cilantro, jalapeno, and pickled onion.

Notes

Mostly hands-off — good for a lazy Sunday that sets up an easy weeknight taco night. Adapted from the recipe at foodnetwork.com.
