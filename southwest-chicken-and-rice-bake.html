Southwest Chicken And Rice Bake

Imported from the family's AnyList recipe collection.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Rice
  • 3 Chicken Breast
  • 1 Small onion
  • 1 cup Thick salsa
  • 1 pkg Taco seasoning
  • 1 can Canned Black Beans
  • 1 cup Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Sour cream

Instructions

  1. Cook rice
  2. Cook and cube chicken
  3. Drain beans
  4. Dice onion
  5. Sauté onion until tender
  6. Add chicken, salsa, taco seasoning, beans
  7. Stir together with cooked rice
  8. Place in baking dish and sprinkle with cheese
  9. Bake at 350 until cheese is bubbly and everything is heated through
  10. Serve with sour cream
