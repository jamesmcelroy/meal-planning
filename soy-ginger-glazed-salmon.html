Soy-Ginger Glazed Salmon

A fast, high-protein weeknight dinner with fresh ginger and a sticky soy glaze. A family favorite.

17 minTotal time 4 servingsYield EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • Sliced scallions, for garnish
  • Sesame seeds, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Whisk together soy sauce, honey, grated ginger, garlic, rice vinegar, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add salmon fillets skin-side up and sear 4 minutes.
  3. Flip salmon, pour glaze over the top, and cook 4-5 more minutes until glaze thickens and salmon is cooked through.
  4. Spoon extra glaze over the fillets and garnish with scallions and sesame seeds.

Notes

A hit the first time it was made — now in the regular rotation. Great with steamed rice or a quick cucumber salad.
