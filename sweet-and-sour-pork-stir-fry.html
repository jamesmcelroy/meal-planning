Sweet and Sour Pork Stir-Fry

Pan-fried (not deep-fried) pork with peppers, onion, and tomato in a quick sweet-and-sour sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from playswellwithbutter.com).

25 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 15 + 10 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs boneless pork loin, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/4 cup rice flour
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil, divided
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 2 large tomatoes, deseeded and diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup rice wine vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1-2 tablespoons soy sauce

Instructions

  1. Season the pork with salt and toss lightly with the rice flour.
  2. Whisk together the brown sugar, rice wine vinegar, ketchup, and soy sauce for the sauce.
  3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the pepper, onion, tomato, and garlic about 1 minute until just tender-crisp, then remove from the skillet.
  4. Add the remaining oil and brown the pork in batches, about 90 seconds per side.
  5. Pour the sauce into the skillet with the pork and cook on high 2-3 minutes, stirring often.
  6. Return the vegetables to the pan, toss to combine, and serve over rice.

Notes

Dredging in rice flour instead of deep-frying keeps this lighter than the restaurant version. Adapted from the recipe at playswellwithbutter.com.
