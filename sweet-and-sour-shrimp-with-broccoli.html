Sweet and Sour Shrimp with Broccoli

Shrimp and broccoli in a ketchup, soy, and rice vinegar sauce, served over rice. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from onceuponachef.com).

30 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 20 + 10 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons ketchup
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 teaspoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon sambal oelek or other chili sauce (optional for kids)
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 bunch scallions, sliced, whites and greens separate
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh ginger
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2 lb extra-large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 1/2 cups broccoli florets
  • Cooked rice, to serve

Instructions

  1. Whisk the ketchup, soy sauce, sugar, chili sauce, cornstarch, and vinegar until smooth, then whisk in the broth.
  2. Heat the oil in a large nonstick pan over medium-high. Cook the white scallion parts, ginger, and garlic about 2 minutes.
  3. Add the shrimp and stir for about 2 minutes, until mostly pink but not fully cooked.
  4. Pour in the sauce, add the broccoli, and stir about 3 minutes until the sauce thickens and everything is cooked.
  5. Top with the green scallion parts and serve over rice.

Notes

Serves 4 to 6 in the source at 2 lb of shrimp; scale down as needed. Adapted from the recipe at onceuponachef.com.
