Tajin Shrimp Tacos

Quick-cooked shrimp with Tajin seasoning, cabbage slaw, and a creamy avocado dressing. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

35 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 20 + 15 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 avocado
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 12 oz raw medium shrimp, peeled
  • 1/2 tablespoon Tajin seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1/2 head red cabbage, shredded (about 4 cups)
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 jalapeno, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Blend the avocado, sour cream, mayonnaise, garlic, cilantro, lime juice, and salt until smooth.
  2. Toss the shredded cabbage with about half of the avocado dressing.
  3. Toast the tortillas in a dry skillet, about 10 seconds per side, and set aside.
  4. Toss the shrimp with the Tajin seasoning. Heat the oil in the skillet and cook the shrimp about 3 minutes until opaque.
  5. Build the tacos with the cabbage slaw and shrimp, top with jalapeno slices, and drizzle with the remaining dressing.
