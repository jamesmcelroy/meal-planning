Teriyaki Salmon with Sriracha Mayo

Salmon marinated in a quick homemade teriyaki sauce, pan-seared, and topped with spicy mayo. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

40 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 + 30 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salmon fillet, cut into 4 portions
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1/2 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, grated
  • 1/2 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons rice wine
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons sriracha

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the soy sauce, water, sesame oil, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, rice wine, and cornstarch to make the marinade.
  2. Place the salmon in a zip-top bag, pour in the marinade, seal, and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
  3. Heat a well-oiled nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the salmon skin-side up 2-3 minutes per side, until it reaches your desired doneness.
  4. Stir together the mayonnaise and sriracha, and spoon over the salmon to serve.

Notes

Good over rice with steamed broccoli. The marinade time is hands-off, so actual kitchen time is closer to 15 minutes. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
