Thai Coconut Curry Chicken Thighs

Chicken thighs braised in a quick red curry-coconut sauce, served over rice. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from budgetbytes.com).

35 minTotal time 5 servingsYield 5 + 30 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 5 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 tablespoons red Thai curry paste
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 (13 oz) can coconut milk
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1/4 teaspoon fish sauce
  • 5 cups cooked jasmine rice, for serving
  • Fresh cilantro, sliced green onion, and lime wedges, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Brown the chicken thighs 3-5 minutes per side, then remove them from the skillet.
  2. Add the curry paste, garlic, and ginger to the skillet and saute about a minute until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the coconut milk, chicken broth, and fish sauce, scraping up any browned bits. Return the chicken to the skillet, cover, and simmer on low 15 minutes.
  4. Serve each piece of chicken with rice and a ladle of the curry sauce, topped with cilantro, green onion, and a lime wedge.

Notes

Start light on the curry paste if you're not sure how spicy it is — easy to add more. Adapted from the recipe at budgetbytes.com.
