Thai Peanut Noodles with Vegetables

Soba noodles with edamame and bell peppers tossed in a peanut-lime sauce. Found via weekly recipe search (originally from foodnetwork.com).

20 minTotal time 4 servingsYield 10 min + 5 minPrep + cook

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup Thai peanut sauce (bottled or homemade)
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1-2 teaspoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 8 oz soba noodles (or whole wheat spaghetti)
  • 6 oz shelled edamame, thawed if frozen
  • 2 red bell peppers, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro leaves
  • Toasted sesame seeds, for serving

Instructions

  1. Whisk the peanut sauce, water, soy sauce, red pepper flakes, and lime juice together in a large bowl.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook the noodles, edamame, and bell peppers together for a few minutes, until the noodles are tender.
  3. Drain, rinse with cold water, and drain well, then toss everything with the peanut sauce.
  4. Top with cilantro and sesame seeds; serve at room temperature.

Notes

Good served slightly warm or cold - makes great leftovers for lunch. Adapted from the recipe at foodnetwork.com.
