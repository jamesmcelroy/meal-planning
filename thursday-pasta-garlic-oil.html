Pasta with Garlic, Olive Oil & Parmesan

A classic Italian pantry pasta — aglio e olio style. Rich, savory, and done in the time it takes to boil water.

20 minTotal time 4 servingsYield EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti or linguine
  • 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup good quality olive oil
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, freshly grated
  • 1/2 cup reserved pasta water
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon salt for pasta water

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add salt and cook pasta until al dente, 1 minute less than the package says. Before draining, scoop out 1/2 cup of pasta water.
  2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook gently, stirring, until garlic is golden but not brown — about 5 minutes. Burnt garlic is bitter.
  3. Add drained pasta directly to the skillet. Pour in a splash of reserved pasta water. Toss vigorously over medium heat until a creamy emulsion forms, adding more pasta water as needed. About 90 seconds.
  4. Remove from heat. Add parmesan and toss. Garnish with parsley if using. Serve immediately.

Notes

The pasta water is the secret ingredient — its starch emulsifies the oil into a silky sauce. Don't skip reserving it. Use the best olive oil you have; it's the star of this dish.
